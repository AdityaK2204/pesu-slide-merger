Slide Merger for PESU Academy

Slides or notes: one PDF per unit, in class order.

How to use

  1. Log in to PESU Academy.
  2. Open My Courses and click a course.
  3. Pick a unit tab, choose Slides or Notes, then click Download Unit N… just below the tabs.

Options

Files go to Downloads › PESU Slides › course name. Every class gets a bookmark in the merged PDF.
